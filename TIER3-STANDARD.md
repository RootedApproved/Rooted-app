# ROOTED — Tier 3 Standard & Decision Log

Working document for the sitewide Tier 3 buildout. Defined by J, applied consistently
across sessions. **147 subcategories currently have zero Tier 3 entries.**

---

## The hard floor — never listed, at any tier

A product containing any of the following is not listed on ROOTED at all. It does not
become a Tier 3 "budget" option. It is simply absent.

- PFAS / PTFE / PFOA
- Parabens
- Phthalates
- Formaldehyde donors (DMDM hydantoin, quaternium-15, imidazolidinyl urea)
- Triclosan
- Oxybenzone / octinoxate
- Undisclosed "fragrance" / "parfum"
- **Aluminum antiperspirant** (decided explicitly — the mainstream deodorant default is
  floor, not Tier 3)

## What Tier 3 actually is

Not a bargain bin. Either of these qualifies:

1. **The mainstream default with its compromise named.** The thing people already reach
   for, listed so the tradeoff is visible. This is the teaching function — Crest and the
   Colgate toothbrush are the model.
2. **A genuinely low price that is still decent.** Real accessibility for someone who
   can't reach Tier 1 pricing.

The defining feature is **one specific, disclosed compromise**, not a low price. Existing
Tier 3 entries include a $98 pant and a $128 dress shirt.

### Explicitly NOT floor — acceptable as a named Tier 3 compromise

Established by the calibration set above: SLS, titanium dioxide, artificial dye,
elastane / polyester / nylon, disposable plastic, silicones, petrolatum, ethoxylated
surfactants (PEG-, -eth), synthetic preservatives (phenoxyethanol, chlorphenesin).

## Verification requirements

Non-negotiable, and the reason this cannot be batch-generated:

1. Full ingredient or material list pulled from a **primary source** — manufacturer site,
   DailyMed, or the product label. Never a blog roundup or an AI recollection.
2. Screened against the full floor list above.
3. The specific compromise identified and stated plainly in the verdict.
4. Product confirmed currently available and not reformulated.
5. Price marked `~$` until J confirms via screenshot.

**If no candidate in a subcategory clears the floor, nothing is added.** An empty Tier 3
is a correct outcome and the chat already reports it honestly ("Nothing in X is rated
Tier 3 — the lowest tier listed there is Tier N"). Forcing an entry to fill a gap is the
one failure mode that actually damages the platform.
